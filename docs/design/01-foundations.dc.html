Design system · v1

A warm, fast system for
one restaurant and its staff.

Nothing here is a framework default. Every neutral carries a trace of brown, actions are pills, containers are 14px, and anything a kitchen argues about — price, quantity, time, order code — is set in mono with tabular figures. Two products share these tokens: a mobile menu the guest holds for four minutes, and a floor tool a waiter holds for eight hours.

07 · Non-negotiables

Rules the system enforces

01One primary button per screen. On the guest phone it is always 54px, full width, in a glass dock.
02Nothing interactive is under 44px, including waiter status buttons used with a thumb while carrying plates.
03Sold-out dishes stay visible and dimmed. Removing them makes guests ask the waiter what happened.
04Guest notes echo in saffron everywhere they appear — cart, kitchen ticket, waiter card, receipt.
05Body text never below 15px on staff screens, 17px for guest reading copy. Contrast at least 4.5:1 on all text.
06Money is mono and tabular. A column of totals must align on the decimal, always.
